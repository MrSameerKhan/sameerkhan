> *"I'm currently at ₹29.28L fixed. I'm looking at ₹35L+ fixed as my expectation — the total CTC can be structured around that."*

> *"If the total CTC is capped, can we restructure it so more of it is fixed? That works better for me without changing your budget."*

> *"My current total is ₹36.6L, with ₹29.28L fixed. I'm targeting ₹42-48L with at least ₹35L+ fixed, depending on the role and growth opportunity."*

## 18. Interview Prep by Company Type

### FAANG / Big Tech (Google, Microsoft, Amazon India)

**Rounds:** DSA (2-3) → ML Design (1-2) → Behavioral (1)

| Round | What They Ask | How to Prepare |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| DSA | Arrays, graphs, DP, sliding window — LeetCode med/hard | 150+ problems, patterns not memorization |
| ML Design | Recommendation system, search ranking, ad CTR | 8-step framework (see 03_LEARNING_PACK §25) |
| ML Concepts | Bias-variance, regularization, loss functions, backprop | 03_LEARNING_PACK §20-21 |
| LLM/GenAI | RAG architecture, fine-tuning tradeoffs, hallucination | 02_INTERVIEW_PACK Part A |
| Behavioral | Leadership, conflict, impact, ownership | 02_INTERVIEW_PACK Part C — prepare 5 STAR stories |

**Tip:** FAANG cares most about DSA + system design. Start LeetCode 3 months before applying.

### Fintech / Banking AI (JPMorgan, Goldman, HSBC, Razorpay)

**Rounds:** Take-home → Technical (1-2) → Hiring manager (1)

| Round | What They Ask | How to Prepare |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| Take-home | Classifier / NLP / extraction system | Have GitHub projects ready |
| Technical | Project walkthrough, production decisions, tradeoffs | Explain every line and choice |
| ML Concepts | Precision/recall tradeoffs, class imbalance, monitoring | Phase Th-1 + MLOps |
| Domain | Financial doc types, compliance, data privacy | Your ICE experience IS the answer |
| Hiring Manager | Why finance, ambiguity, team fit | 3 stories of cross-functional impact |

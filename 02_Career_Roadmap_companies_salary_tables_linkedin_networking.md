### Negotiation Scripts

**When recruiter gives you an offer:**
> *"Can you share the fixed vs variable breakup? I'd like to understand how much of this is guaranteed."*

**When pushing for higher fixed:**
> *"I'm currently at ₹29.28L fixed. I'm looking at ₹35L+ fixed as my expectation — the total CTC can be structured around that."*

**When they say the budget is fixed:**
> *"If the total CTC is capped, can we restructure it so more of it is fixed? That works better for me without changing your budget."*

**When they ask your current CTC:**
> *"My current total is ₹36.6L, with ₹29.28L fixed. I'm targeting ₹42–48L with at least ₹35L+ fixed, depending on the role and growth opportunity."*

## Interview Prep by Company Type

**Rounds:** DSA (2–3) → ML Design (1–2) → Behavioral (1)

| Round | What They Ask | How to Prepare |
|-------|--------------|----------------|
| DSA | Arrays, graphs, DP, sliding window — LeetCode medium/hard | 150+ problems, focus on patterns not memorization |

**Tip:** FAANG cares most about DSA + system design. ML concepts are secondary. Start LeetCode 3 months before applying.

|--------|--------------|-------------|
| ML Engineers at target companies | LinkedIn → filter by company + title | See script below |
| Hiring managers (not HR) | LinkedIn → search "[Company] ML Lead / Head of AI" | Direct, specific ask |
| Founders of Document AI startups | Twitter/X + LinkedIn | Comment on their posts first, then DM |
| Indian ML community | Weights & Biases Discord, Hugging Face forums, AI India Slack | Be helpful first, self-promote second |

### LinkedIn outreach message (copy-paste template)
```
Hi [Name],

I noticed you work on [X] at [Company] — I've been building similar systems
(Document AI + LLM pipelines for financial docs at ICE, previously Al Rajhi Bank).

I'm exploring new opportunities and would love 15 minutes to learn about
how your team approaches [specific problem]. Happy to share what I've built too.

No pressure at all — just genuine curiosity.
[Your name]
```

**Rules:**
- Personalize line 2 every time — mention something specific about their work
- Never ask for a job in first message
- Follow up once after 5–7 days if no reply
- 10 outreaches/week consistently beats 50 in one week
